# Plan: the Observer marketplace (developers who won't share an API)

Decided with the account owner on 19 Nov 2026, after research into how inventory moves in Dubai today.

## Principles
- **Observer is a marketplace between buyers, brokers and developers.** It stays software: licensed partner brokerages own every client and take the commission. Observer earns subscriptions and success fees.
- **No developer API is assumed.** Developers keep Observer current because it brings them qualified buyers and saves their sales admin hundreds of broker calls.
- **Developer portals are automated only with that developer's written agreement.** Everywhere else Observer relies on price lists, WhatsApp, aggregators, public data and the developer reply desk.
- **Client money never passes through Observer or the broker.** Deposits go to the developer or the escrow account.
- **Advertising rules apply.** The AI answers clients who asked; it does not push listings unprompted. Off-plan marketing follows each developer's no-objection letter and Trakheesi (DLD advertising permit) rules.
- **Markets:** UAE and Saudi Arabia (REGA, FAL and Wafi rules for Riyadh).
- **Developers in the demo:** Emaar is the permitted workspace. Other developers (Sobha, DAMAC and others) appear by name as **market data**, with sample units labelled illustrative and no claim of partnership.

## Stage 1: availability confidence and the midnight deal pack
- **Confidence score:** every unit gets a score built from:
  - the last price list;
  - developer broadcasts;
  - DLD registrations;
  - aggregator feeds;
  - portal checks, where agreed;
  - how fast the project sells.
- **Quoting at night:** the AI quotes a price only at 80% confidence or higher. Below that, it says it is confirming first thing and offers the two closest units with higher confidence.
- **Overnight, the AI may:**
  - quote with the confidence level and the time of the last confirmation;
  - collect the passport and proof of funds on WhatsApp, read them automatically and pre-fill the booking form;
  - send the developer's own refundable deposit (EOI) link;
  - queue a hold request for the moment the developer's desk opens, with the client's place in line.
- **Resale:** only units with a signed Form A at a partner brokerage. Nobody is contacted unprompted.

## Stage 2: developer reply desk and developer AI
- **Updating without integration:** the developer's sales admin can send a WhatsApp message ("A-1204 sold"), use a no-login web link per project, or forward the price-list email. Every broker sees the change.
- **Developer AI:** it answers brokers' questions overnight from the developer's latest inventory and rules. A person approves holds.

## Stage 3: deal room and AI-to-AI requests
- **Deal room:** when a client commits, one thread opens for the client, the broker and the developer's salesperson, with the full history, as the developer's desk opens.
- **AI-to-AI requests:** structured hold requests go from the broker's AI to the developer's AI or desk. Both sides see the request, its queue position and the answer.

## Stage 4: data feeds and demand radar
- **Feeds:**
  - DLD open data (Dubai Pulse daily transactions and the off-plan flag);
  - aggregator partnerships (Alnair, Reelly or similar, by agreement);
  - Form A resale stock;
  - developer WhatsApp groups (messages forwarded to the AI).
- **Demand radar:** anonymised totals for developers ("312 qualified buyers asked for a 2-bed in Creek Harbour under AED 2.5M this week"). It shows counts and ranges only, never names or contacts, and uses only clients who agreed to data use.
