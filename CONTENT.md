# Observer AI: voice and copy

## Voice
**Premium, calm, specific.** Picture the most trusted senior broker in Dubai: unhurried, precise, never selling too hard.

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Numbers over adjectives: "four minutes", not "lightning fast".
- Talk about the buyer and the agent, not about "AI" or "the platform".
- Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps labels. No exclamation marks.
- Never: *revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge, unlock, leverage, game-changer, supercharge, empower*.
- Buttons are a verb and an object: "Book a demo", "Send a shortlist", "Confirm the viewing".
- Errors explain what happened and how to fix it. They don't apologise.

## Arabic
- Modern Standard Arabic as used in Gulf business, not a literal translation of the English.
- Same meaning and rhythm, written to read naturally in Arabic.
- Numbers stay in Western digits (as Gulf property portals use them); currency is "درهم".
- Never letter-space Arabic. Line height is a little more generous (1.3 on display text).
