# Client feedback plan: developer selling through many brokers

The client is a developer that sells through many outside brokers. Their feedback, with decisions agreed on 19 Nov 2026.

## What "lagging" means (all four)
1. **The app feels slow.** Measure every demo screen on desktop and a mid-range phone, then fix the slowest until each responds in about a second.
2. **Replies reach clients late.** Response time is shown on every channel and alerts fire when the target is missed.
3. **Inventory goes out of date.** Every unit shows when its data was last confirmed. When data is older than 24 hours, the AI confirms with sales admin before quoting.
4. **Brokers report back late.** Brokers update clients, holds and deals through the portal or WhatsApp. Inactivity counts against protection (below).

## Stage 1: brokers, client protection and unit holds
- **Broker portal plus WhatsApp:** register clients, see live availability, request holds, track commission, and get WhatsApp alerts.
- **Client protection:** the first broker to register a client keeps them for **30 days**, renewed by any logged call, viewing or offer. With no activity, the client is released.
- **Duplicate checks:** the same phone number or email across portals, brokers and the developer's own channels is matched and flagged.
- **Disputes:** clashes go to a dispute queue for the developer's sales team, with the evidence timeline.
- **Unit holds:** timed (24–48 hours) and released automatically unless a deposit arrives. Other brokers can queue behind a hold. Two brokers can never hold the same unit.
- **One price list:** every broker sees the same current price and plan.

## Stage 2: inventory without an API, and data freshness
- **Ways in:** a CRM sync where possible, an Excel or PDF price list read by AI, a forwarding email address, a developer broker-portal export, a shared Google Sheet, and sales admin marking units by hand.
- **Freshness:** every unit has a "last confirmed" time.
- **Stale data:** when data is older than 24 hours, the AI tells the client it is confirming availability, asks sales admin, and replies once it's confirmed.

## Stage 3: launch controls (too many clients)
- Online expression of interest and refundable booking deposits.
- A waitlist and timed callbacks booked by the AI.
- People handle the top-scored clients only; the AI handles the rest until they qualify.
- Qualified leads the team can't reach overflow to registered brokers by set rules.
- Launch planning accounts for WhatsApp limits: approved templates, daily limits on new conversations, and sender quality.

## Stage 4: payment calculator and GCC Intelligence
- **Calculator:** rebuilt with rules per country (mortgage limits, registration fees and taxes). Rental yield is shown separately from any assumed appreciation, and nothing is presented as a guaranteed return.
- **GCC Intelligence:** all six GCC countries. Only verified figures appear, each with its source and date. Anything that can't be verified is left out, not estimated.

## Stage 5: Emaar workspace and speed
- **Emaar workspace:** Emaar is shown as the workspace on the public site. The user confirms Emaar's written permission.
  - All figures are sample data, labelled "Sample data" on every screen.
  - Agent and client names are fictional.
- **Speed:** the measure-then-fix pass described above.
